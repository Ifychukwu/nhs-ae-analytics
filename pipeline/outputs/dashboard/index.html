Method and data checks

  • Every run first checks that provider rows add up exactly to NHS England's published England totals, that no counts are negative or duplicated, that 4 hour breaches never exceed attendances, and that monthly history adds up to the published annual totals. The run stops if any check fails.
  • Demand model: ordinary least squares on log attendances per day, with a linear trend and a month-of-year effect. Underlying trend 2018-23: — a year; in-sample error —. July to September 2026 ran — above the projection, so the missing 2023-26 months should be added before relying on the forecast.
  • ECDS measures (admitted within 4 hours, 12+ hours in A&E) are published as experimental and come straight from provider submissions; they can differ from MSitAE for the same trust.
  • Admission rate was left out of the clustering because it reflects case mix and local counting more than pressure.
